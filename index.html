Data

Columns: year,usd_inr,sector,imports,exports. One row per year per sector, values in US$ billion, usd_inr = yearly average. Sector keys are in data.js.

Data: trade by fiscal year (2025 = FY2024-25), goods from Ministry of Commerce TradeStat, services from RBI, USD-INR from World Bank (fiscal-year rate approximated from calendar years). Mineral fuels include coal and gas; services "Software, Business & Other" is RBI's Miscellaneous category. INR value = USD value × rate. Change % = (new − baseline rate) ÷ baseline rate. Balance = exports − imports. Volumes are held constant (no elasticity or J-curve modelling).
